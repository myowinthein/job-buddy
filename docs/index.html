Free and open source, using your own Gemini key.

First, connect AI

Bring a free Gemini key

Job Buddy uses Gemini to read resumes and understand form questions that fixed rules cannot resolve. You bring your own API key, so there is no Job Buddy subscription or account to create.

Gemini’s free tier is a practical starting point for everyday applications. How far it goes depends on your forms, model and Google’s current limits; it is not an unlimited allowance.

  1. Open Google AI StudioSign in with Google and create an API key. A free-tier key does not require setting up paid billing.
  2. Save the key in Job BuddyOpen Settings → AI Features, paste your key, and select Save Key. Job Buddy checks it and finds a working model.
  3. Choose a model when you need toThe model control lets you switch between the supported Flash-Lite and Flash models. Your key and model preference stay in this browser.

Flash-Lite for speed

Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite is the default candidate. Google positions it for fast, high-volume tasks, making it a useful first choice for routine extraction and matching.

Flash for harder questions

Gemini 3.8 Flash offers stronger reasoning for complex tasks. It may take longer; switching models does not guarantee that every form will be filled.

What happens when a model is busy or a limit is reached?

Requests try the selected model first. On supported temporary failures, including timeouts, rate limits or an unavailable model, Job Buddy can try the other supported model. Invalid credentials and unrelated documents need your action instead. Changing keys in the same Google project does not create a new quota.

Where can I check usage?

Use View usage and limits in AI Features to see your usage and quota in Google AI Studio. Limits vary by model and project. If you reach a limit, check when it resets in AI Studio.

Google references: model capabilities, free-tier setup, usage limits. Checked November 5, 2026.

Then, save your details

A profile you can keep using

Type your details yourself, import a resume, or combine the two. Your profile is organized into nine sections, so you can revisit one part without working through everything again. Save each section after editing.

  • Personal: name, contact details and background
  • Address: city, country and postal address
  • Work Authorization: status by country
  • Work History: summary, experience and notice period
  • Education: institutions, qualifications and dates
  • Languages: languages and proficiency
  • Links: LinkedIn, portfolio, GitHub and custom links
  • Documents: CV and cover letter files or links
  • Salary: expected and current pay

Completion is a checklist

The percentage tracks 10 required checks, using your saved data. At 100%, required details are complete; optional details can still be added. Last name, salary, LinkedIn, work history and personal background details do not increase the percentage.

Select the remaining-details button at the top right to see what is missing. Select a section or field to go directly to the relevant input.

You can start before 100%

Autofill needs a saved first name, a valid email, and a Gemini key. More profile information gives it more to work with. Missing information is left for you to enter on the form.

Documents support saved files and URLs. You can view a PDF or download a saved attachment. Upload and import files are limited to 4 MB each, within the browser’s total storage capacity.

Skip the retyping

Import a resume, then decide

With your Gemini key saved, choose Import Resume and upload a PDF or DOCX, up to 4 MB. Job Buddy checks the file, sends it to Gemini, and compares the extracted details with your saved profile locally.

You get to choose what enters your profile. A resume is a starting point: check names, dates, locations and any information it does not contain.

Keep Current
Close the import and leave your saved profile as it is.
Import All
Apply the incoming details without choosing each change separately.
Review Changes
Work through the sections and choose which details or entries to import. New entries show incoming data; changed entries let you compare both versions.
A possible different person
A name or email mismatch can prompt Update or Replace. Update keeps omitted details; Replace starts fresh with your selected import and clears omitted profile data, documents and mappings.

Resume updates keep missing details in uniquely matched entries and retain saved entries omitted from the resume. Replacing a profile keeps local appearance and Gemini settings, but pauses connected Drive backups for review.

What if the document is not a resume?

Unrelated documents are rejected. If the document type is uncertain, Job Buddy asks you before continuing. A file extension alone is not enough to pass the local file checks.

Now, open an application

Fill the form. Keep the final say.

Open an application page, select the Job Buddy icon in Chrome’s toolbar, and choose Fill Form. Pin the extension in Chrome if you want to reach it more easily.

If the form already has answers, choose Fill empty fields to preserve them, or Fill all matched fields to also replace existing answers that Job Buddy can match. Unmatched fields remain yours to complete.

Closing the popup does not stop an accepted fill request. Job Buddy continues on the source page; reopen the popup to see progress or answer a pending choice. Refreshing, navigating away or closing the form cancels the run.

The colors tell you where to look

Green
Filled with high confidence, or confirmed by you.
Yellow
Filled, but needs your review.
Red
Not filled: no safe match, or the form rejected the value.
Gray
Not filled: a profile match exists, but no data is saved.

Consent questions are skipped, and existing answers stay unchanged in empty-fields mode. Review all answers and attachments before submitting on the employer’s website. Job Buddy never submits for you.

Missing key or profile details

The popup points you to Settings or the profile inputs you need. A key, first name and valid email are required to start a new fill. You can still manage your profile and backups without a key.

No fields detected, or the page cannot be reached

Open the application form itself, let it load, and try again. Refresh a page that was already open when the extension was installed or updated. Chrome’s internal pages and some custom or closed-shadow controls cannot be filled.

AI is unavailable, or only part of the form is filled

Accepted rule-based fills remain. Complete the remaining fields yourself, check the key or model in Settings, or try again later. The warning explains an actual AI failure, rather than treating every unfilled field as an AI error.

Undo, interruptions and the previous fill

Undo restores the values recorded before filling where possible, while preserving your later manual edits. Some custom controls cannot be restored completely. After an interruption, accepted writes and available Undo are retained. Results are associated with the source form, rather than a different tab you happen to open.

One less click next time

Keep Fill Form beside the form

Quick Fill places a movable Fill Form control on pages that look like job applications. It uses the same autofill process as the toolbar popup.

Choose how it fills

In Settings → Quick Fill, use Empty fields only to preserve existing answers, or Empty and existing fields to fill all matched fields. Empty fields only is the default. You can turn the floating control off entirely.

Move it or hide it

Drag the handle to move it. Its menu can hide it until refresh or hide it for that site. To show it again, find the site in Settings → Quick Fill → Hidden sites and select Show.

Position, fill mode and hidden sites stay saved in this browser across restarts. Hiding or moving the control does not cancel a fill already running.

If the floating control does not appear on an unusual form, use the Chrome toolbar popup. After a fill, Undo and View details are available from the control.

When you want to look closer

Follow each field through the fill

Tools → Fill Details shows the latest selected fill report. View details in the popup or floating control takes you there; the report is recorded even if you never press that button.

It updates while you edit the source form. A new nonempty fill replaces the report. View details on another open form can select its report again. This is a record of filling, not a history of submitted applications.

How an answer reaches a field

  1. Learned mappings

    Try a trusted correction previously saved for this site and question.

  2. Rules

    Look at browser autocomplete hints, labels, identifiers and nearby context to match a profile field.

  3. Gemini

    Review eligible fields that still need help, including uncertain rule matches. High-confidence AI may refine a yellow rule fill.

A reliable result can finish the process early. Not every field reaches every stage, and a successful match still needs a value that the form accepts.

Read the summary and filters

Detected fields are divided into Filled, Not filled, Kept current and Skipped. A choice group counts once. Filled can be green or yellow; Not filled can be red or gray. Skipped covers excluded questions such as consent. Kept current means an existing answer was preserved.

Search titles and readable profile paths, then narrow the results by outcome, color or final source. Checked by rules and Sent to AI count processing stages, so they can overlap. Final source counts identify which stage or manual action supplied the final fill.

Read a field’s expanded stages

Select Learned mappings, Rules or AI to see the recorded decision at that stage. Rules show the method, original match score and profile field. Learned mappings show trust and confirmation information. AI shows qualitative confidence, not an invented percentage. A match can still fail if the form rejects the write.

What do the rule methods and percentages mean?
Method How it helps
Autocomplete attribute Uses a standard browser hint such as given-name. Usually scores 95%.
Exact dictionary match Matches a known field label or alias. Normally scores 85%.
Similar field label Allows similar wording; the score varies with similarity and safeguards.
Custom link label Matches one of your named custom links.
Current-status checkbox Uses a restricted set of known current-work or current-study terms.
Nearby context Uses surrounding question text when the direct signals are insufficient.

Rules fill at 60% or higher and use green at 85% or higher. These are matching scores, not measured probabilities of correctness. Option matching or a rejected write can change the final result. Trusted learned mappings use a separate trust signal; Gemini confidence remains High or Low.

What happens when the source form closes?

The last report stays for this browser session. Return to Form focuses the original open tab. If it has closed or navigated away, Open Form opens the saved address in a fresh tab, without restoring answers. Reset and profile replacement clear the report; JSON and Drive backups never include it.

Make repeated corrections count

Teach the questions you meet again

After autofill, you may need to correct an answer. If the value uniquely matches information saved in your profile, Job Buddy can remember which profile field you used for that question. Type the correction, then move to another field to record it.

One recorded confirmation makes a mapping Learning. Confirm the same match again and it becomes Trusted, ready for future fills on that site. Autofill alone, or focusing and leaving a field without editing it, does not teach a mapping.

Learning
One manual confirmation. It is not trusted for autofill yet; a conflicting correction starts learning the new match.
Trusted
Two confirmations, or an explicit edit in this page. Eligible trusted mappings take priority over rules and AI.
Inactive
Saved but excluded from filling, for example when a referenced profile entry was removed or became ambiguous.
Edit and delete
The pencil changes the destination using the profile-field chooser. Saving trusts your choice immediately. The bin removes a mapping after confirmation; a site-level bin removes that site’s mappings.

Review what has been learned

Review Mappings uses Gemini to check selected sites. It sends saved questions and profile-field names, then presents better matches, unclear matches and unavailable fields for your decision. You review changes before applying them.

Older mappings can be repaired automatically

When you fill a form, Job Buddy can repair known issues in older mappings when the form provides clear evidence. Unchanged work is not repeated; changed mappings, new evidence or a newer repair version can be checked again. These repairs do not use Gemini or silently choose a different profile field. Use Review Mappings when you want AI to check the pairing.

Take it with you

A profile is a file you can move

Settings → Export Profile downloads a Job Buddy JSON file containing your profile, learned mappings and safe export metadata. Use it to keep a copy or move to another browser or device.

Settings → Import Profile reads that file, checks its structure and compares it with the saved profile. An empty profile imports valid data directly; identical data leaves it unchanged. Differences use the same Keep Current, Import All and Review Changes flow as resume import.

JSON and Drive updates reconcile imported learned mappings with saved ones. Local conflicting choices are kept and matching snapshots do not add confirmations. Replace adopts the selected incoming data and mappings. JSON and Drive review apply accepted incoming lists; resume updates additionally retain saved entries omitted from the resume.

The field catalog is part of Job Buddy’s code: it gives fields shared labels, ordering and references across the editor, chooser and review screens. Your personal values live in the profile JSON in this browser’s local extension storage. An export includes the values and mappings, not your Gemini key, appearance or Drive connection.

For an automatic copy

Back up to your own Google Drive

Connect Google Drive in Settings to save your profile and learned mappings in Drive’s private app-data area. No Job Buddy server sits between your browser and your Google account.

If Drive is empty, Job Buddy creates a backup. Matching data is reconciled. If the backup differs, review it before continuing, using the same shared comparison controls. A possible different person also gives you the Update or Replace choice.

After saving changes
A profile or mapping save queues backup after about five seconds. Backups follow saved changes rather than a fixed timer.
Back Up Now
Check and update the backup manually. Temporary failures can retry automatically; an expired authorization may need Reconnect.
Disconnect
Keep the last successful backup, or choose to delete it. Disconnect does not first upload pending edits; use Back Up Now if you want a current copy.
Imports and exports
Saved imports normally queue backup while connected. A local profile replacement pauses Drive for review. Export only downloads a file; it does not trigger a backup.
Keep Current means something different in Drive review

Explicitly keeping this browser’s profile uploads that profile to Drive. Closing or cancelling the dialog only dismisses the review and leaves uploads paused. Replacement from a resume or JSON also pauses backups so you can decide what to do with the existing Drive copy.

Drive backs up profile data and learned mappings. It excludes Gemini credentials, appearance, Quick Fill preferences, connection tokens and Fill Details reports.

The data stays yours

Keep control of what you share

Job Buddy is free, open source, and has no ads, analytics or Job Buddy account. The extension stores your profile and settings in this browser. There is no Job Buddy backend holding your applications.

AI features do make requests to Google. Resume import sends the selected document and extracted links. Autofill sends relevant form questions and profile details, excluding stored document files. Background details are disclosed only when the question asks for them. AI mapping review sends saved questions and profile-field names. Google’s own service and data-use terms apply.

Data Where it goes
Profile and learned mappings Local extension storage; JSON when you export; Google Drive when connected.
Gemini key and model preference Local settings. The key authenticates requests to Gemini; it is excluded from profile backups.
Appearance and Quick Fill preferences This browser only.
Fill Details One metadata-only report for the browser session, outside profile backups.

Start over when you need to

Settings → Reset All Data clears the local profile, documents, learned mappings, settings and retained Fill Details. It removes the Gemini key, disconnects Drive, resets appearance and Quick Fill preferences, and requires you to type DELETE.

If Drive is connected, choose whether to delete its backup too. Leaving that choice off keeps the remote backup. If a requested backup deletion fails, reset stops so you can retry. Keep an export first if you want your data back later.
