Free and open source, using your own Gemini key.

Set up Gemini

Start with a key from Google AI Studio . Gemini offers a free tier, with limits that depend on your model and Google project. No Job Buddy account is needed.

Job Buddy uses AI to improve autofill accuracy, import resume details, and suggest corrections to learned mappings. A Gemini key is required for these features.

Which model, and what happens at its limit?

Flash-Lite is the default for speed. Flash is an alternative for more demanding questions. Job Buddy tries your selected model first, then another supported model after a timeout, rate limit or temporary failure.

Check Google’s model information and current limits.

Build your profile

Enter your details manually or import them from a resume. Autofill uses what you save, and you can start before 100% completion with a saved first name, a valid email address and a Gemini key.

Your profile reaches 100% completion once all required fields are filled and saved. Optional details don’t affect progress, but give autofill more information to use.

Import your resume

Save time on manual entry by extracting profile details from your resume. Review the information before choosing what to keep.

Job Buddy rejects unrelated documents and asks for confirmation when it is unsure whether a document is a resume. A PDF or DOCX file extension alone is not enough.

What if the resume belongs to someone else?

If the name or email differs from your saved profile, you may be asked how to import.

  • Update: Add selected details and keep the rest of your profile.
  • Replace: Start fresh with selected details, clear learned mappings, and pause connected Drive backups for review.

Fill a form

Pin Job Buddy in Chrome for easier access. On a job application form, open the Job Buddy popup and click Fill Form.

Autofill highlights fields with these colors:

  • Green: Filled with high confidence or confirmed by you.
  • Yellow: Filled, but needs your review.
  • Red: Not filled because no safe match was found or the form rejected the value.
  • Gray: Not filled because the matching profile field has no data.

Job Buddy leaves consent questions for you to answer and never submits the application. Review your answers and attachments before submitting.

What if Job Buddy can’t find or access the form?

Open the application form itself, wait for it to load, and try again. If the page was already open when Job Buddy was installed or updated, refresh it first. Chrome’s internal pages and some custom controls cannot be filled.

What if AI is unavailable or some fields stay empty?

Any answers already filled remain in place. If the popup shows an AI error, check your Gemini key, selected model and usage limits in Settings, or try again later. You can fill any remaining fields manually.

Use Quick Fill

Quick Fill appears on detected job forms. Click Fill Form to autofill without opening the popup.

Drag the handle to move Quick Fill. Its position, fill mode and hidden sites stay saved in this browser across restarts.

Read fill details

Your latest autofill results appear here and update as you edit the application.

Autofill tries up to three stages for each field. It stops early when it fills a reliable answer.

  1. Learned mappings reuse trusted matches from your earlier corrections.
  2. Rules match browser hints, field labels and nearby context to your saved profile.
  3. AI uses Gemini to match remaining questions to your profile and improve uncertain rule-based answers.
How do learned mappings fill form fields?

Learned mappings remember corrections you make after autofill using saved profile details, so the same questions are easier to fill next time.

  • First correction: The mapping becomes Learning.
  • Second matching correction: The mapping becomes Trusted.

Job Buddy first checks for a mapping for that site and question. If it is active and Trusted, it uses the current profile value. Otherwise, it continues to Rules.

Scores show match strength, not accuracy. Only the displayed percentages are rounded.

  • 85% or higher: Rules can fill the value as green.
  • 60% to below 85%: Rules can fill it as yellow for your review.
  • Below 60%: Rules leave it unfilled and pass it to AI.
How does AI match form fields?

AI handles fields that learned mappings and rules couldn’t fill confidently.

Job Buddy sends the question, input details, nearby text and available choices to Gemini, along with saved profile fields and their values.

Gemini responds in one of three ways:

  • High confidence: AI can fill the value as green.
  • Low confidence: AI can fill it as yellow for your review.
  • No match: AI leaves the field unchanged.

Learn from corrections

As explained in how learned mappings work, corrections that match your profile are recorded here after autofill for you to review and manage.

Some older mappings contain incomplete questions or generic text such as “Enter your answer.” When you autofill that site again, Job Buddy restores full questions where the match is clear and marks generic mappings Inactive so they won’t autofill.

What does Review Mappings do?

Manual corrections can still point to the wrong profile field. Review Mappings uses Gemini to check saved mappings from your selected sites.

It shows mappings that need attention and suggests a better profile field or making the mapping inactive when none fits. You review the suggestions before applying any changes.

Import and export

Save your profile, documents and learned mappings as a JSON backup to restore later or use in another browser or device.

Your Gemini key and model, Drive connection, appearance, Quick Fill preferences and Fill Details report stay in this browser and aren’t included in JSON exports.

What if the imported profile belongs to someone else?

You’ll see the same Update or Replace choices as resume import.

Connect Google Drive

While connected, Job Buddy backs up your profile, documents and learned mappings about five seconds after the latest saved change.

Your Gemini key and model, Drive connection, appearance, Quick Fill preferences and Fill Details report stay in this browser and aren’t included in Drive backups.

What if the backup belongs to someone else?

You’ll see the same Update or Replace choices as resume import.

Reset your data

This deletes your profile, documents and learned mappings from this browser. Export a backup first if you want to keep a copy.

Appearance and Quick Fill return to their defaults. Your Gemini key is removed and Google Drive is disconnected. You can keep or delete the Drive backup.
