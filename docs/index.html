Keep your profile in your browser. Let Job Buddy fill the familiar questions, then review your answers before you submit.

Free and open source, using your own Gemini key.

Set up Gemini

Start with a key from Google AI Studio. Gemini offers a free tier, with limits that depend on your model and Google project. No Job Buddy account is needed.

Which model, and what happens at its limit?

Flash-Lite is the default for speed. Flash is an alternative for more demanding questions. Job Buddy tries your selected model first, then another supported model after a timeout, rate limit or temporary failure.

Check Google’s model information and current limits.

Build your profile

Your saved profile supplies the answers. Add it by hand, from a resume, or both.

You can start before 100% completion. Autofill needs a saved first name, a valid email address and a Gemini key.

Import your resume

A resume gives you a starting point. Nothing changes until you accept the import.

Job Buddy rejects unrelated documents and asks for confirmation when it is unsure whether a document is a resume. A PDF or DOCX file extension alone is not enough.

What if the resume belongs to someone else?

If the name or email differs from your saved profile, you may be asked how to import. Update adds the selected details and keeps the rest. Replace starts fresh with the selected details and clears current documents and learned mappings.

Fill a form

Pin Job Buddy in Chrome for easier access. On a job application form, open the Job Buddy popup and select Fill Form.

Autofill highlights fields with these colors:

  • Green: Filled with high confidence or confirmed by you.
  • Yellow: Filled, but needs your review.
  • Red: Not filled because no safe match was found or the form rejected the value.
  • Gray: Not filled because the matching profile field has no data.

Job Buddy leaves consent questions for you to answer and never submits the application. Review your answers and attachments before submitting.

No fields detected, or the form cannot be reached

Open the application form and wait for it to load before trying again. Refresh the page if it was open when Job Buddy was installed or updated. Chrome’s internal pages and some custom controls do not support autofill.

AI is unavailable, or only part of the form is filled

Answers already filled stay in place. If the popup reports an AI error, check your Gemini key, model and usage limits in Settings, or try again later. You can complete any remaining fields yourself.

Use Quick Fill

Quick Fill appears when Job Buddy detects a job application form. Select Fill Form on the floating control to start autofill without opening the toolbar popup.

Drag the handle to move Quick Fill. Its position, fill mode and hidden sites stay saved in this browser across restarts.

Read fill details

Your latest autofill results appear here and update as you edit the application.

Autofill tries up to three stages for each field. It stops early when it fills a reliable answer.

  1. Learned mappings reuse trusted matches from your earlier corrections.
  2. Rules match browser hints, field labels and nearby context to your saved profile.
  3. AI uses Gemini to match remaining questions to your profile and improve uncertain rule-based answers.
What do the counts mean?

Filled includes green and yellow. Not filled includes red and gray. See the color explanations under Fill a form.

Kept current means an existing answer was preserved. Skipped includes excluded questions such as consent. A radio or checkbox question group counts once.

Final sources identify where each filled answer came from. Checked by rules and Sent to AI count processing stages, so they can overlap. Search covers question titles and readable profile paths.

How do rule methods and match scores work?

Rules connect a question on the form to a field in your saved profile. The method tells you which clue was used. The percentage is Job Buddy’s rating of that match.

Method shown How it finds a match
Autocomplete attribute The website identifies the input as an email, name or another standard field.
Exact dictionary match Recognizes a known label, such as “First name,” and connects it to your saved First Name.
Similar field label Compares wording when a label is not an exact match, including small spelling differences.
Custom link label Matches a question to the label of a custom link you saved, such as “Behance.”
Current-status checkbox Recognizes a current-job or current-study checkbox and checks the corresponding profile entry.
Nearby context Uses text around an input when its own label does not identify the profile field.

A website hint normally scores 95%, while a known label normally scores 85%. Similar wording and surrounding context can receive lower scores. The percentage is not a probability that the answer is correct.

  • 85% or higher: Rules can enter the saved value as green.
  • 60%–84%: Rules can enter it as yellow for your review.
  • Below 60%: Rules do not enter a value. AI may still find a usable match.

Learn from corrections

  1. Correct an answerEdit a field after autofill, then leave it.
  2. LearningOne confirmation of a saved profile value.
  3. TrustedTwo confirmations. Used on future fills.

The correction must uniquely match a value in your profile. Autofill alone, or focusing a field without editing, does not teach a mapping.

Automatic repair and AI review do different jobs

Automatic repair uses clear evidence from the form to fix known mapping issues locally. It does not silently choose a different profile field. Unchanged work is not repeated; changed mappings, new evidence or a newer repair version can be checked again. Review Mappings asks Gemini to check the pairing itself.

Import and export

Your JSON copy carries stored documents and learned mappings along with the profile.

A possible different person adds the same Update or Replace choice as resume import. Replace clears omitted data and pauses connected Drive backups for review.

Connect Google Drive

Saved profile and mapping changes queue a backup after about five seconds.

What happens during a Drive review?

Explicitly choosing Keep Current uploads this browser’s profile to Drive. Closing or cancelling only dismisses the review and leaves uploads paused. Saved imports normally queue a backup; a local profile replacement pauses it for review.

What if a backup stops working?

Temporary failures can retry automatically. If Google authorization expires, reconnect the account. Disconnecting keeps the last successful backup unless you choose to delete it.

Your data and reset

Free and open source, with no ads, analytics or Job Buddy backend. Your profile and settings live in this browser.

In this browser
Profile, files, learned mappings, Gemini settings, appearance and Quick Fill preferences.
With Gemini
Resume import sends the selected document and extracted links. Autofill sends relevant questions and profile details, excluding stored document bytes; background details are included only when asked. Mapping review sends questions and field names.
In your backups
JSON and optional Drive backups include profile data, stored documents and learned mappings. They exclude credentials, appearance, Quick Fill preferences and Fill Details reports.

When you want to start over

Reset removes the Gemini key and disconnects Drive. A kept Drive copy can be restored later; requested backup deletion must succeed before local reset continues.

Export a copy first if you want to keep your data. Google’s own service and data-use terms apply when you use Gemini or Drive.
