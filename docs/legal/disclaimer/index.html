Autofill and AI Disclaimer

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Review before submitting

Job Buddy is a form-filling tool developed by Myo Win Thein. It matches information from your saved profile to a website's questions. A filled field or confidence highlight is not a guarantee that the answer is correct, complete, current, or accepted by the application platform.

Review every field, selected option, date, salary amount, work-authorization answer, and uploaded document before submitting an application. A website can validate, save, or upload filled information before you press Submit. Job Buddy cannot control the employer's form or what happens after information reaches it.

AI suggestions can be wrong

Optional Gemini features can misunderstand a résumé or question, omit information, or suggest an incorrect match. Review extracted profile changes before saving and review AI-filled answers on the application page. Job Buddy does not independently verify your qualifications, eligibility, employment history, or supporting documents.

Use Google's API only where its age, region, account, and data-use requirements permit your use. In particular, personal résumé or profile information must not be sent to a Gemini service whose terms prohibit it. Your own API key does not by itself make a request private or prevent provider retention or human review. The privacy policy explains what the current implementation sends.

No hiring or compatibility guarantee

Job Buddy does not make hiring decisions and cannot guarantee an interview, an offer, a successful submission, or compatibility with every website. Website changes, unsupported controls, missing profile data, storage limits, and network or provider failures can prevent a fill or backup from succeeding. Keep your own copies of important documents and application information.
